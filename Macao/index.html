♠ Card Game ♥

Goal: Be the first to empty your hand.

On your turn you may play one or more cards onto the play pile, or draw a card from the draw pile.

Playing a card: a card can be played if it matches the suit or value of the top card on the pile. You may play multiple cards of the same value at once — tap to select, then hit Confirm Play. The first card selected must legally match the pile; the others ride along.

Special cards:
 • 2 or 3 — forces the opponent to draw that many cards. Punishment stacks: they can counter by playing their own 2 or 3, passing the full total back to you.
 • 4 — skips the opponent's next turn. They can counter by playing their own 4, bouncing the skip back. Multiple 4s played together stack the skips additively.
 • Ace — wild card, plays on anything. After playing one you choose the active suit.

Drawing: if a draw punishment is active, clicking the draw pile absorbs the full stacked total. If a skip is pending and you have no 4, clicking draw accepts the skip and passes the turn.
